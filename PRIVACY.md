Privacy Policy

Beauty of Fractals does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data with
the developer or third parties.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, a developer
account system, location services, camera, microphone, or contacts.

Fractal rendering and PNG export happen locally on the user's device. Exported
images are created only when the user asks for them.

Favorite spots can optionally sync through the user's Apple iCloud account. On
iPhone, the paired Apple Watch app can receive render progress, preview images,
and zoom information through Apple WatchConnectivity. These Apple system
services are used only for app features and are not connected to a developer
server.

For support, contact: mac@applem1.de
