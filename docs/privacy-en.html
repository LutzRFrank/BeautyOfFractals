Privacy Policy

Beauty of Fractals does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, a developer account system, location services, camera, microphone, or contacts.

Fractal rendering and PNG export happen locally on your device. Exported images are created only when you ask for them.

Favorite spots can optionally sync through your Apple iCloud account. On iPhone, the paired Apple Watch app can receive render progress, preview images, and zoom information through Apple WatchConnectivity. These Apple system services are used only for app features and are not connected to a developer server.

For privacy questions, contact mac@applem1.de.
