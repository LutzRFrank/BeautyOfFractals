We’re happy to help

Support

If something does not work as expected, please email mac@applem1.de.

Helpful details for troubleshooting

  • Your device and operating system version
  • Your version of Beauty of Fractals
  • A short description of the steps you took
  • If possible: a screenshot, fractal type, zoom level, and iteration count

Ideas and reproducible issues

You can also open a public GitHub issue. Please do not share personal information there.

iCloud and Apple Watch

To sync favorite locations, iCloud must be enabled on your devices. The Watch app communicates with the paired iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework.
