# App Review Notes

Do not store credentials, private contact details, or other secrets in this file.

## Version 2.8 — macOS

Thank you for reviewing Beauty of Fractals.

- No account, sign-in, subscription, or purchase is required.
- The app does not collect user data and does not require network access.
- All fractal rendering and PNG export are performed locally.
- Use the controls at the bottom of the window to select a fractal mode, palette, render quality, and iteration depth.
- Zoom by clicking the magnifying-glass controls or by interacting directly with the fractal. Pan by dragging.
- Press Command–Shift–C, or choose Fractal > Show / Hide Controls, to toggle the controls.
- Export options are available from the Export menu in the controls. Some Ultra Export options are intentionally unavailable at extreme deep-zoom coordinates where the normal high-precision export path must be used.
- Optional render diagnostics and status panels can be shown from the controls or the Fractal menu.

## Version 2.8 — iPhone and iPad

Thank you for reviewing Beauty of Fractals.

- No account, sign-in, subscription, or purchase is required.
- The app does not collect user data and does not require network access.
- All fractal rendering and PNG export are performed locally on the device.
- Use the controls to select a fractal mode, palette, render quality, and iteration depth.
- Navigate with standard touch and pinch gestures.
- The controls can be collapsed to provide an unobstructed fractal view.
- Export options are available from the Export control. Some Ultra Export options are intentionally unavailable at extreme deep-zoom coordinates where the normal high-precision export path must be used.
- Longer high-precision renders display progress and may be cancelled from the render status interface.
