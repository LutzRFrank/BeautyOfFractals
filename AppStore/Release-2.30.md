## macOS — en-US

### Subtitle

Explore fractals with popovers

### Keywords

Mandelbrot,Julia,math,art,zoom,geometry,palettes,visualization,PNG,patterns

### Promotional text

Explore fractals with a cleaner Mac workspace: native toolbar actions, compact settings popovers, precise deep zooms, and local PNG export.

### Description

Beauty of Fractals turns mathematical structures into an immersive visual
journey on your Mac. Version 2.30 brings a cleaner workspace with native toolbar
actions and compact popovers for fractal selection, quality, iterations, and effects.

Explore the Mandelbrot set and a diverse collection of related fractals with
smooth navigation, fast Metal rendering, precise CPU deep zooms, expressive
palettes and abstract rendering styles, iCloud-synced favorite spots, and local
high-resolution PNG export.

FEATURES

- Explore Mandelbrot, Power of n (2-12), Julia, Burning Ship, Tricorn, Celtic,
  Newton, Kleinian, Mandelbrot Relief, Mandelbulb 3D, Mandelbox 3D, and Eight
  Rainbows
- Navigate fluidly with zooming and panning
- Use Automatic precision selection from fast interactive views through guarded
  Extreme Precision
- Reveal selected Mandelbrot structures around 10^50x, with deeper views
  available as an experimental frontier
- Render difficult deep zooms with adaptive reference anchors and coverage
  repair
- Let the app choose an iteration budget from the center orbit plus a reserve,
  or select up to 250,000 iterations manually
- Save and revisit precise favorite spots with thumbnails, palette, quality,
  mode, and effective iteration counts
- Sync favorites through iCloud across Mac, iPhone, and iPad
- Follow longer renders on Apple Watch through the paired iPhone app
- Create abstract worlds with Metallic Dreams, Embossed Metal, Guilloché,
  Spheroid Glass, Quantum Glass, Quantum Traps, and Orbital Waves
- Shape the new styles with dedicated controls for structure, complexity, curl,
  relief, engraving, glass, interference, and waves
- Choose from additional distinctive color palettes, including Aurora, Auric,
  Pearl, Rainbows, and Deep Current
- Export true high-resolution PNG images recalculated locally for the selected
  output size
- Open selection and effect settings on demand from toolbar icons for an
  unobstructed fractal view
- Inspect render status and advanced diagnostics when you want to see what the
  precision engine is doing

Beauty of Fractals is designed for exploration. There are no accounts,
advertisements, analytics, or tracking. Rendering and export happen locally on
your Mac. Optional iCloud sync and Apple Watch communication use Apple system
services.

### What’s New

A clearer workspace for fractal exploration:

• Native toolbar actions replace the large floating control panel.
• Open Fractal, Skin & Quality and Iterations & Effects in compact popovers.
• Keep navigation, favorites, help, render status, diagnostics, Iteration Journey, and all existing export options within reach.
• Show renderer information during the live Metal preview as well as progress during deep-zoom calculations.
• Enter exact iteration budgets directly in the settings popover.

## TestFlight — What to Test (English)

Test both settings popovers: mode, skin, quality, power exponent, iterations, plateau tilt, and all applicable effect sliders. Check exact iteration entry, range limits, invalid input, and slider synchronization. Check that values update the fractal and popovers stay open while adjusting sliders. Check Undo, zoom, Reset, favorites, help, render status, and PNG export.

On iPhone and iPad, check portrait and landscape, the zoom label below system UI, popover scrolling, and toolbar overflow at constrained sizes. On Duo, check closed and fully opened displays and transitions between them. Dedicated partially folded layouts are outside the advertised scope.

On Mac, also test keyboard shortcuts, exact viewport copy/paste, diagnostics, Iteration Journey, and normal/Ultra/Dual Monitor/Overscan export. Verify menus and popovers in the standard window and fullscreen. On a paired Apple Watch, check render progress and final preview.

## TestFlight — Testhinweise (Deutsch)

Bitte beide Einstellungs-Popovers prüfen: Typ, Skin, Qualität, Potenz, Iterationen, Plateau-Neigung und die jeweils verfügbaren Effektregler. Genaue Iterationseingabe, Bereichsgrenzen, ungültige Eingaben und die Synchronisierung mit dem Slider testen. Änderungen sollen das Fraktal aktualisieren; Popovers sollen beim Verstellen der Regler geöffnet bleiben. Undo, Zoom, Reset, Favoriten, Hilfe, Renderstatus und PNG-Export testen.

Auf iPhone und iPad Hoch- und Querformat, die Zoom-Anzeige unterhalb der Systemleisten, scrollbare Popovers und den Toolbar-Überlauf bei wenig Platz prüfen. Auf Duo das geschlossene und vollständig geöffnete Display sowie Größenwechsel testen. Für teilgefaltete Ansichten wird kein eigenes Layout beworben.

Auf dem Mac zusätzlich Tastenkürzel, Kopieren/Einfügen exakter Ansichten, Diagnostik, Iteration Journey sowie normale, Ultra-, Dual-Monitor- und Overscan-Exporte testen. Fenster und Vollbild prüfen. Auf einer gekoppelten Watch Fortschritt und Vorschaubild testen.
