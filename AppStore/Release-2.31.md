## macOS — en-US

### Subtitle

Explore mathematical art

### Keywords

Mandelbrot,Julia,math,art,zoom,geometry,palettes,visualization,PNG,patterns

### Promotional text

Explore fractals with a compact selection bar, clearer Quick Start tips, expressive palettes, precise deep zooms, and local PNG export.

### Description

Beauty of Fractals turns mathematical structures into an immersive visual journey on your Mac. A compact selection bar keeps fractal type, skin, and quality within reach, while revised Quick Start tips help you find your way.

Explore the Mandelbrot set and a diverse collection of related fractals with
smooth navigation, fast Metal rendering, precise CPU deep zooms, expressive
palettes and abstract rendering styles, iCloud-synced favorite spots, and local
high-resolution PNG export.

FEATURES

- Explore Mandelbrot, Power of n (2-12), Julia, Burning Ship, Tricorn, Celtic,
  Newton, Kleinian, Mandelbrot Relief, Mandelbulb 3D, Mandelbox 3D, and Eight
  Rainbows
- Navigate fluidly with zooming and panning
- Use Automatic precision selection from fast interactive views through guarded
  Extreme Precision
- Reveal selected Mandelbrot structures around 10^50x, with deeper views
  available as an experimental frontier
- Render difficult deep zooms with adaptive reference anchors and coverage
  repair
- Let the app choose an iteration budget from the center orbit plus a reserve,
  or select up to 250,000 iterations manually
- Save and revisit precise favorite spots with thumbnails, palette, quality,
  mode, and effective iteration counts
- Sync favorites through iCloud across Mac, iPhone, and iPad
- Follow longer renders on Apple Watch through the paired iPhone app
- Create abstract worlds with Metallic Dreams, Embossed Metal, Guilloché,
  Spheroid Glass, Quantum Glass, Quantum Traps, and Orbital Waves
- Shape the styles with dedicated controls for structure, complexity, curl,
  relief, engraving, glass, interference, and waves
- Choose from additional distinctive color palettes, including Aurora, Auric,
  Pearl, Rainbows, and Deep Current
- Export true high-resolution PNG images recalculated locally for the selected
  output size
- Show or hide the compact fractal selection bar and open iterations and effects
  in a dedicated popover
- Inspect render status and advanced diagnostics when you want to see what the
  precision engine is doing

Beauty of Fractals is designed for exploration. There are no accounts,
advertisements, analytics, or tracking. Rendering and export happen locally on
your Mac. Optional iCloud sync and Apple Watch communication use Apple system
services.

### What’s New

A clearer start and easier access to your fractal settings:

• Show or hide the compact selection bar for fractal type, skin, and quality. The app remembers your preference.
• Find the Power exponent alongside iterations and effects.
• Get started with revised Quick Start tips, clearer icons, and German or English guidance.
• Open supported direct links to Mandelbrot, Julia, Burning Ship, and Eight Rainbows.

## App Review — English

### macOS

Thank you for reviewing Beauty of Fractals 2.31 (76).

No app account, sign-in, subscription, or in-app purchase is required. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated backend. Fractal rendering and PNG export happen locally. Optional favorites sync uses the user's Apple iCloud account.

Version 2.31 updates the Quick Start guidance with German and English instructions, clearer icons, and improved text alignment. This update introduces no additional data collection.

Direct-link support is included for the following destinations:
beautyoffractals://event/explore
beautyoffractals://event/julia
beautyoffractals://event/burning-ship
beautyoffractals://event/eight-rainbows

To test, put a link into Notes and open it with this build installed. The link selects the destination's default viewport, palette, and 300 base iterations. It also works while the app is already running. Ordinary navigation remains available afterward. Unknown routes are ignored. Link support does not depend on an active App Store event, a login, or a remote service.

The gauge button toggles render status. Ordinary views use Metal; supported deep zooms use high-precision CPU rendering. Extreme-zoom results depend on location and iteration budget.

On Mac, the selection toolbar button or Command-Shift-C shows or hides the compact bottom bar for fractal type, skin, and quality; this preference is remembered. The sliders button opens Iterations & Effects, including the Power exponent when a Power fractal is selected. Click outside to dismiss the popover.

Drag a rectangle to zoom and Option-drag to pan. Undo, zoom, Reset, favorites, help, diagnostics, Iteration Journey, and the existing PNG export options remain available. Command-Shift-P toggles render status. Command-S exports a 2560 × 1600 PNG. The Help button opens the welcome and Quick Start view.
