## macOS — en-US

### What’s New

Your creative controls, closer to the fractal:

• Open Iterations & Effects directly from the bottom selection bar. The popover appears just above its button.
• The controls stay visible while the lower effects popover is open.
• After you collapse the selection bar, its handle fades away after 10 seconds, leaving an unobstructed view.
• Hover over the visible handle to keep it available, or reopen the controls at any time using the toolbar button or Shift-Command-C.

### Promotional Text

Explore fractals with iterations and effects within reach in the bottom bar. Hide the controls for a clear view, then return to creating with one click.

### Description

Beauty of Fractals turns mathematical structures into an immersive visual journey on your Mac. A compact selection bar keeps fractal type, skin, quality, iterations, and effects within reach. Collapse it for a clear view; its handle fades away after a short delay. Revised Quick Start tips help you find your way.

Explore the Mandelbrot set and a diverse collection of related fractals with
smooth navigation, fast Metal rendering, precise CPU deep zooms, expressive
palettes and abstract rendering styles, iCloud-synced favorite spots, and local
high-resolution PNG export.

FEATURES

- Explore Mandelbrot, Power of n (2-12), Julia, Burning Ship, Tricorn, Celtic,
  Newton, Kleinian, Mandelbrot Relief, Mandelbulb 3D, Mandelbox 3D, and Eight
  Rainbows
- Navigate fluidly with zooming and panning
- Use Automatic precision selection from fast interactive views through guarded
  Extreme Precision
- Reveal selected Mandelbrot structures around 10^50x, with deeper views
  available as an experimental frontier
- Render difficult deep zooms with adaptive reference anchors and coverage
  repair
- Let the app choose an iteration budget from the center orbit plus a reserve,
  or select up to 250,000 iterations manually
- Save and revisit precise favorite spots with thumbnails, palette, quality,
  mode, and effective iteration counts
- Sync favorites through iCloud across Mac, iPhone, and iPad
- Follow longer renders on Apple Watch through the paired iPhone app
- Create abstract worlds with Metallic Dreams, Embossed Metal, Guilloché,
  Spheroid Glass, Quantum Glass, Quantum Traps, and Orbital Waves
- Shape the styles with dedicated controls for structure, complexity, curl,
  relief, engraving, glass, interference, and waves
- Choose from additional distinctive color palettes, including Aurora, Auric,
  Pearl, Rainbows, and Deep Current
- Export true high-resolution PNG images recalculated locally for the selected
  output size
- Show or hide the compact fractal selection bar and open iterations and effects
  directly above it or from the toolbar
- Inspect render status and advanced diagnostics when you want to see what the
  precision engine is doing

Beauty of Fractals is designed for exploration. There are no accounts,
advertisements, analytics, or tracking. Rendering and export happen locally on
your Mac. Optional iCloud sync and Apple Watch communication use Apple system
services.

## App Review Notes — English

Thank you for reviewing Beauty of Fractals 2.32 (77) for macOS.

This update improves access to existing controls. Open the bottom selection bar with the selection toolbar button or Shift-Command-C. The bar now includes an Iterations & Effects button that opens the existing settings directly above the lower button. The toolbar effects button remains available and opens its own popover at the toolbar. Only one effects popover is shown at a time.

The expanded selection bar stays visible. When the bar is collapsed and the lower effects popover is closed, its handle waits 10 seconds, then fades out over two seconds. Hovering the visible handle pauses the delay; leaving it starts a fresh delay. Hidden controls do not intercept clicks. Reopen the selection bar at any time through the toolbar button or Shift-Command-C. The lower effects popover is not dismissed by the fade timer.

No app account, sign-in, subscription, or in-app purchase is required. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated backend. Rendering and PNG export happen locally. Optional favorites sync uses the user's Apple iCloud account. No additional data collection is introduced.

Existing navigation, favorites, render status, diagnostics, Iteration Journey, PNG export, and the event deep links are retained. Direct-link example: beautyoffractals://event/julia. Unknown routes are ignored. The update does not announce or schedule an App Store event.

This is a macOS-only update. The iOS app and Watch companion remain at 2.31 (76).
