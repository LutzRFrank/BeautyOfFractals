# Privacy Policy for Beauty of Fractals

Beauty of Fractals does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data with
the developer or third parties.
The app does not require an account or sign-in.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or
third-party data collection services.

Fractal rendering and PNG export happen locally on the user's Mac. Images
exported by the user are not uploaded or shared by the app.

Favorite spots can optionally sync through the user's Apple iCloud account.
This uses Apple's iCloud infrastructure only for the app feature and is not
connected to a developer-operated server.

If you have any questions, please contact:
